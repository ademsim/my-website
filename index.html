About Myself

I was born in 1979 in Istanbul, Türkiye. I completed my high school education in a technical high school specializing in computer technologies. For my undergraduate studies, I pursued a degree in Computer Engineering in Cyprus and graduated with honors. I then obtained my master's degree in Information and Communication Systems Security from KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm, Sweden. Upon returning to Türkiye, I worked for three years as an Information Security Consultant at Uyumsoft, one of the country's largest software companies. During this period, I also completed my Ph.D. at Yaşar University in İzmir, focusing on the detection of advanced persistent threats. In 2013, I co-founded ITSAFE, a consultancy firm providing services in information security, international compliance, and IT standards. Since 2020, I have been teaching information security courses at Istanbul Aydın University and Belek University in Antalya.

Academically, I deliver undergraduate and graduate-level courses on Cryptography, Software Security, AI Security, Network Security, and Introduction to Information Security. In addition to my academic and professional work, I actively participate in various NGOs in leadership and mentorship roles, particularly in the area of social responsibility. I also take part in both national and international projects, and continue to share my knowledge with younger generations by teaching coding and robotics. As a hobby, I am involved in initiatives related to both traditional and hydroponic agriculture. Professionally, I am open to national and international collaborations in the areas of cybersecurity, advanced threat analysis, and global compliance frameworks.
